PocketPeshi — Legal Case Diary

SADEVZ (product) · 2026 · Full-stack web app

Live
Overview

“Your case diary, in your pocket.” A mobile-first case-management app for advocates and their assistants in Pakistan. A monthly calendar shows hearing density and flags cases with missed proceedings; each case carries a chronological proceedings history, litigation-stage mapping (appeals, revisions, executions), fee tracking with running balances, and audit trails for voided entries. It works offline — a proceeding recorded with no signal syncs on reconnect — and lets users photograph and file order sheets and pleadings directly with the case. Role-based for admins, lawyers and assistants.

Challenge

Modelling the real procedural movement of Pakistani litigation — appeals, revisions, remands, executions — as structured data, and making note-taking reliable inside courthouses with no connectivity through an offline-first sync layer with visible pending status.

Stack
Next.jsTypeScriptTailwind CSSPrismaPostgreSQLSupabaseCapacitorVercel
