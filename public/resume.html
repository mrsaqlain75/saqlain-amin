Independent Products

PocketPeshi

Private use

PocketPeshi rebuilds the lawyer's traditional case diary as a mobile-first app. The home screen is a live calendar — opening any date surfaces that day's cases, so a lawyer or their assistant can record what happened at each proceeding and fix the next hearing date right from the entry, including miscellaneous and interim proceedings that don't fit a single case's timeline. Built as a role-based system for admins, lawyers, and assistants, so a whole chamber can run its diary from one shared tool instead of paper registers.

Madina Home Textile

Live demo

An ecommerce storefront for a home textile retailer selling curtains, foam, carpets, and related household items. Shoppers browse the catalog and check out directly on the site, while a purpose-built admin panel lets the owner manage products, track orders, and read sales analytics without a spreadsheet. A lightweight built-in CMS handles the storefront's content — banners, categories, and promotional copy — so the shop can update itself without a developer.
