How it's built

Questions

Common questions

Why Next.js instead of Shopify or WooCommerce?

Hosted platforms are great to start, but you pay monthly, you're limited to their themes and apps, and you don't own the stack. A Next.js store is yours — faster, fully customisable, and cheaper to run at scale. If a hosted platform is genuinely the better fit for you, I'll say so.

Can customers in Pakistan pay easily?

Yes — Stripe for international cards, plus local payment and cash-on-delivery flows where the business needs them.

Can I manage products myself?

That's the point of the admin dashboard and CMS — you add products, run promotions and fulfil orders without me.

How long does a store take?

A focused first version is usually 3–6 weeks depending on catalog size and how custom the checkout needs to be.
